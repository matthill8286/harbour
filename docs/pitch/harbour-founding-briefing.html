The mess

The car is expensive. The system around it is sticky tape.

An import desk does not usually buy the car and sell it on. It collects it, registers it, moves it, and hands it to the owner. When something goes quiet, the whole day becomes phone calls.

Today it looks like this

  • The job list lives in a spreadsheet
  • The customer lives in WhatsApp
  • The tracker lives in another login the customer must not see
  • What it cost you and what you charged them sit in the same mess

Then this happens

  • The owner asks “where is my car?” and nobody has a clean answer
  • Staff cannot see which jobs are actually stuck this morning
  • You move the tracker onto the next car and lose the old trail
  • Number plates get typed in by hand, from a website or a photo
Who uses it: the people running the desk. Who watches: their customer, on a simple page that never shows costs, suppliers, or the number on the tracker box.

How your money sits

Later, if the desk is actually using it, they would pay each month like any other work tool. That is a later conversation. Right now I need enough to make the first version exist.

If it works, roughly

  • A busy month — about €400

Plain rules

  • Your €3,000 is the start-up money, not rent
  • Trackers are ordinary boxes you buy separately. I am not making gadgets
  • More cars through the books can mean a higher monthly bill later
  • Those monthly numbers are a sketch, not a price list

Nothing is for sale until there is a product. I am not asking you to buy a subscription.

The order of events

Build it. Use it. Then we talk again.

There is no product yet, and no money coming in. That is why I am writing to you.

1. Start

I build the first version: jobs, a customer page, a map, start a job from a plate or a photo. UK to Cyprus only.

2. Prove it

Keep what the desk actually uses. Drop what they ignore. Success is fewer phone calls, not a pretty demo.

3. Then we talk

If it is in daily use, we sit down about what comes next. I am not asking you to fund that now.

I am not asking you to believe in a finished app. I am asking you to fund the first version, with a real desk in the room.
